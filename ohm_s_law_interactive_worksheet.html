Ohm's Law Fundamentals

Interactive practice for Voltage (E), Current (I), Resistance (R), and Power (P)

Variables & Units

  • E

    Voltage (Electromotive Force)

    Measured in Volts (V)

  • I

    Current (Intensity)

    Measured in Amperes/Amps (A)

  • R

    Resistance

    Measured in Ohms (Ω)

  • P

    Power

    Measured in Watts (W)

Common Formulas

To find E (Voltage)

E = I × R

E = P / I

To find I (Current)

I = E / R

I = P / E

To find R (Resistance)

R = E / I

R = E² / P

To find P (Power)

P = I × E

P = I² × R
